The money you want to earn is already in someone else's hands.

For someone to give you their money, you have to give them something they believe is worth paying for. You either sell something they need, or provide a service that solves a problem for them.

That's why having a valuable skill matters. A skill gives you something you can develop, offer to people, create opportunities with, and ultimately, earn from.

  • How can I make more money?

Over time, I developed the skill, put it into practice, worked with clients, and turned what I knew into a source of income. I've since watched other people begin their own journey the same way.
